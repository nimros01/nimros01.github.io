Hi, I'm Nimrod. I'm a senior technical expert and algorithms team lead in the CTO office at
[Mobileye](https://www.mobileye.com), where I work on decision-making and planning for autonomous
driving. Before that I was a physicist and theoretical neuroscientist: a PhD at the Hebrew
University of Jerusalem and a Swartz postdoctoral fellowship at Harvard's Center for Brain
Science, modeling learning and memory in neural networks.

What ties it together is a love for complex, chaotic and stochastic systems, and for turning
them into models and simulations that actually run. Outside work I build small apps for myself
and my family, hike in the desert, bike, cook and read (mostly science fiction).
