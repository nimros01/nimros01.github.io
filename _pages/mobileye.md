**Senior technical expert and algorithms team lead, CTO office, Mobileye** · 2021 – present

I joined Mobileye's CTO office in October 2021 as an algorithm developer, working on how an
autonomous car decides what to do next. For the past year I have been a senior technical expert
and team lead of an algorithms team.

## Leading the team

As team lead I am responsible both for my team's algorithms and for the people who build them. I
also interview candidates and mentor new team members through onboarding and their first
projects.

## Decision-making and planning

My core area is decision-making and planning for autonomous driving: turning what the car
perceives into safe, smooth driving behavior. The tools are a mix of optimization, filtering and
other classical methods, combined with the outputs of deep learning perception models.

## Drivable path prediction, from idea to 200,000 cars

I built a drivable path prediction system from the ground up: the first proof of concept in
Python, large-scale data analysis to understand where it works and where it fails, and finally the
production C++ implementation. It is deployed in about 200,000 customer cars.

## Deep learning for vision

I have worked on several deep learning vision projects, such as vehicle type detection and
trajectory prediction, covering the whole path from data preparation through modeling and training
to deployment.

## Mapless driving

I oversee the subject of mapless driving across the company: driving well where no detailed map
is available. That means defining what the perception teams need to provide and implementing the
driving policy that uses it.

## Working across the company

Much of the job happens between teams. I work with groups across Mobileye to integrate the outputs
of perception models into decision-making, and to agree on the requirements that connect the two.
